# STS v5 bouwplan (herziening 2): Uithangbord op de nachtstad, met het bord als onderwerp van de camera

Gegenereerd door de strategie-workflow (14 agents: audit, capaciteiten, kooppsychologie, inspiratie, 4 concepten, 3 juryleden, synthese, criticus, herziening). Dit is het bouwplan voor de cloud-bouwploeg. Nooit onder docs/ plaatsen.

## Gekozen concept

Uithangbord: jouw naam op de stad (buyer-lens winner), built on Het Kwartier's engineering spine, revised after critique so that the one thing is actually legible and the plan is buildable headlessly. The front of the site is the Uithangbord: one unlit shop sign on the existing HERO landmark of the Three.js night city, framed close by the camera (the sign is the subject of the shot, not a prop on the skyline), the question 'Hoe heet je zaak?', eight sector chips in the buyer's words (radio inputs styled as chips), a gemeente field, and two plain-HTML arrival lines that say what STS is before any canvas: 'STS Digital, Antwerpen. Websites, gratis gereedschap en onderzoek naar AI die werk overneemt van kleine zaken.' and 'Een verkenner zoekt, een bouwer maakt het voorbeeld, Snymis leest elk bericht zelf.' The honesty sentence has two variants (typed vs arrived by card/wijk link) and never claims the fictional neighbours are 'op jouw naam'. One persistent strip (name chip + five doors in buyer language + the single action 'Bericht aan Snymis') replaces nav, rail, hamburger, footer-Site column and the nine CTAs. Native scroll everywhere; the city reacts to state (sector colour on the landmark, sign text, one camera keyframe per section in view, 1.2 s interruptible lerp), never to scroll position; the landmark is solid from frame 0 and every progress-driven prop (wire, scan, beacon, belt, trail) is gone. The sign text is always a DOM element (Panchang, crisp at any DPR, identical in the SVG route) positioned by camera.project once per landing; the CanvasTexture plane behind it carries the glow. The body is the Kwartier's architecture: rooms built once from src/rooms/*.html into both an index section and a canonical page, render-on-demand with rust = zero frames on every profile and coverage pause at 60 %, no Three.js on phones or in lite, an inline SVG skyline as the no-WebGL route, flat panels without backdrop-filter, fictional businesses keep fictional names in every simulation (the visitor's name is context only), a frozen cross-page conversion contract (STSBericht.prefill + ?onderwerp=<key>) created in the state core before any room is built, and a QA harness with a forbidden-text scan whose 'jullie' rule is scoped to STS's own voice. Doors: Jouw gevel (index#gevel), De telefoon om 19 u (index#telefoon), Doorlichting (simulatie.html, address kept), Gereedschap (gereedschap.html, address kept), Hoe wij werken (werkwijze.html, new: pipeline replay, de straat met drie panden, eerlijke capaciteitenkaart with the assistant proef under In onderzoek, wie Snymis is). Action: Bericht aan Snymis (index#bericht, the only form).

## Waarom dit wint

Weighted scoring (buyer x2, craft x1, risk x1): Uithangbord 8/6/6 = 28, Het Kwartier 7/8/7.5 = 29.5; with buyer x3 they tie at 36 vs 36.5. The judges disagree on surface, not on substance: the buyer judge objected to the Kwartier's seven metaphor labels, seven mandatory camera flights before any proof, and its phone collapse into an abstract map; the craft and risk judges objected to Uithangbord's conventional structure, its real-name-on-invented-charts move, six-sector copy load, a 16 h scene package with props and a pointer light, and a default-on self-credit. Every one of those objections is removable without losing the other side's strengths, so the synthesis keeps what the buyer lens rewarded (the first ten seconds in the owner's language, native scroll, buyer-named doors, the honesty sentence, the A6 toog card with prefilled QR, prefilled messages in the owner's voice, the 4-second neighbour fallback) and keeps what craft and risk rewarded in the Kwartier (city as living state display with the existing HERO landmark as the sign carrier, rooms as fragment+page, render-on-demand and coverage pause, SVG fallback instead of a SwiftShader-rendered poster, no backdrop-filter over the canvas, fictional names stay fictional, forbidden-text QA, legal dates in front matter, panden.json with consent). The critique then exposed where the plan would have failed in the hands of headless cloud agents, and this revision fixes each: (1) the sign had no world size, camera distance or legibility test and would have been a 120 px smear at the old skyline altitude; now places.start and places.gevel are close frames on the landmark, the sign plane is >= 12 units wide, the text is a DOM element mirrored from camera.project, and an acceptance projects the plane's corners and demands >= 420 x 60 px at 1440x900; (2) WP-03 never said the landmark must be solid from frame 0; now city.update(t, px) bakes lit/heroGrow/heroSolid to 1 and deletes wire, scan, beacon, belt and trail; (3) five parallel room packages depended on STSBericht.prefill created in WP-11; now a frozen 40-line contact-prefill.js contract with a fixed key list lives in WP-02 and every room package depends on it; (4) the 'jullie' lint rule collided with the plan's own owner-voice prefills and customer chips; now it is scoped to STS voice via data-voice; (5) the 'real GPU' Long-Task gate and the raw-byte 1.1 MB budget were unmeasurable or failed on day one; now budgets come from measurement + 10 % with 'without Three' as the hard gate and the Long-Task check is a founder-run step with ?debug=1; (6) the Kawa proef attributed invented behaviour to a real prototype; now it sits under In onderzoek as 'Zo zou een assistent kunnen antwoorden (ontwerp, gescript)' and the Kawa lamp says only what the founder said; (7) the arrival text read as a web agency; now it names research, agents and Snymis in the first viewport with a QA assertion; (8) the honesty sentence was false on card and wijk arrivals; now STSState records the source and the sentence has a variant; (9) invented halves of sts-world/webshot, the 'doorlichting as a service', the 'real template' claim, the declined-example policy and the Moneypenny footnote are cut or moved to sakiMustSupply; (10) the JSON-LD description and priceRange that no package owned are now fixed in WP-01 and the index title in WP-04; (11) the telefoon CTA prefilling an auto-reply into the customer's wa.me opener is replaced by a per-sector customer opener; (12) the coarse-profile tests were vacuous; now there are three named profiles (phone, desktop, tablet) with one table of what loads and what rust renders. The result is still one thing a kapper in Berchem understands in ten seconds, one thing a weak GPU can render (now with rust at zero frames and bloom only after a probe), and one thing one person can maintain.

## Het verhaal 'wat we doen'

STS Digital is een jong bedrijf uit Antwerpen. Eén persoon, Snymis, en een paar agents die hem helpen. Geen kantoor, geen verkopers. Hij doet onderzoek naar AI die werk overneemt van kleine zaken, en gebruikt dat onderzoek elke dag voor zijn eigen werk. Zo werkt het vandaag: een verkenner zoekt in Vlaanderen zaken die klanten 's avonds op hun gsm niet of slecht vinden. Een bouwer maakt met enkel publieke gegevens (je Google-profiel, je openingsuren, je adres) een werkend voorbeeld van een site. En dan stopt de machine: Snymis leest elk bericht dat naar een zaak vertrekt en keurt het goed of niet. Er vertrekt niets dat hij niet gelezen heeft. Dat is geen slogan; het staat zo in onze privacyverklaring.

Wat je vandaag van ons kunt krijgen: een website op maat, geen sjabloon, waarvan je eerst gratis een werkend voorbeeld ziet en pas daarna beslist, met een vast bedrag op papier en jij als eigenaar van domeinnaam en inhoud. Bij dat voorbeeld hoort een eerlijke blik op je Google-profiel en op hoe je huidige site op een gsm laadt; de Doorlichting op deze site toont hoe we zo kijken, op verzonnen zaken. En gratis gereedschap dat in je browser blijft: een zichtbaarheidscheck, een WhatsApp-knop met QR-code voor aan je kassa, een Google-voorbeeld van je titel. Prijzen staan niet op de site, want elke zaak is anders.

Wat nog geen product is, noemen we zo. Prototype (draait bij ons, niet bij klanten en niet op deze site): Kawa, een assistent die lokaal draait en onthoudt wat hij leert; sts-world, een 3D-overzicht van het werk van de agents; webshot, dat een site op een gsm fotografeert. In onderzoek: antwoorden buiten de uren met je echte uren, reviews beantwoorden in jouw toon met jouw goedkeuring, een offerte-opzet uit een foto of spraakbericht, en jouw probleem als we het nog niet kennen. Wat wij niet zijn: geen team van tien, geen klantenlogo's die we niet mogen tonen, geen tellers die niet kloppen. Wel iemand van hier die eerst bouwt en dan pas praat.

## Simulaties

### Jouw gevel: typ je naam op het uithangbord (index#gevel)
- Wat de klant test: His own business name lit on the city, close enough to read, his sector colour on the landmark, and a first sketch of his site on a phone with switches for the three things a customer needs at 19 u.
- Mechaniek: Inputs: zaaknaam (text, 40-char cap via names.js, font-size 16px, no autofocus on touch), sector as <input type=radio> in a <fieldset> styled as chips (horeca, kapper, garage, bakker, aannemer, praktijk, winkel, anders), gemeente (datalist with Antwerp districts and nearby gemeenten first). On each input the DOM sign (#sign-dom, Panchang, two-line wrap at 16 chars) updates immediately and the CanvasTexture plane redraws debounced 120 ms; the HERO landmark gets the sector colour through one emissive uniform; without WebGL the same #sign-dom sits over the inline SVG skyline with a static text-shadow. The camera frames the sign from places.start and places.gevel (14-18 units from the landmark top; projected sign box >= 420 x 60 px at 1440x900). On commit (Enter, button 'Hang mijn bord op', or 1.5 s idle) the camera lerps 900 ms to places.gevel, the strip chip fills, the honesty sentence variant renders by STSState.source, and the phone sketch (preview.js) renders with the name typed in. Four switches (Belknop, WhatsApp-knop, 'Nu open', Uren) toggle data-el blocks and are encoded in the g= bitmask of share links; an 'Om 19 u' toggle dims the frame and outlines Bel/WhatsApp/uren with the sentence 'Dit zijn de drie dingen die een klant om 19 u zoekt.' Preset lines in the sketch show only what the free example really holds (naam, uren, adres, bel/WhatsApp, reviews placeholder 'Jouw echte Google-reviews komen hier' with outlined stars, foto placeholder) and each example line carries 'voorbeeld'. If nothing is typed after 4 s: 'Of kijk mee met een buur' with three fictional neighbour chips (name + '(verzonnen)'), plus 'Liever eerst rondkijken' that scrolls to #buren. State goes to sessionStorage sts-zaak; 'Deel deze schets' builds an explicit URL with ?zaak&sector&gemeente&g= and the Flemish share text. A visually hidden <p id=sign-sr> mirrors the sign text for AT.
- Databron: Only what the visitor typed + eight sector presets in preview-data.js (Flemish example copy limited to the free example's real contents). No external call, no lookup of real businesses.
- Emotionele taak: Pride and recognition (my name, lit and readable, in a city) and relief (small, understandable, I control what is on it). Wonder from the city doing something for me.
- Conversiestap: Button 'Laat Snymis het echte voorbeeld bouwen' calls STSBericht.prefill({key:'gevel'}) which composes 'Dag Snymis, ik ben {zaak} uit {gemeente} ({sector}). Ik zag de schets met belknop en WhatsApp. Mag ik een gratis voorbeeld met mijn echte gegevens?' and scrolls to #bericht.

### De telefoon om 19 u: een dinsdag in een verzonnen zaak van jouw sector (index#telefoon)
- Wat de klant test: What happens to calls on an ordinary working day while the owner serves, kneads, cuts hair or lies under a car, and what two small measures change in that fictional day.
- Mechaniek: Inputs: sector (from sts-zaak, changeable), a 07:00-20:00 timeline that plays in 40 s (one GSAP timeline, pause on hidden/off-screen, 1x/2x, play/pause, skip), and a three-way mode radio: 'Niemand neemt op' / 'Bericht met terugbelbelofte' / 'WhatsApp-knop + bericht met de echte uren'. Per sector a fixed script (sim-dag-data.js) of 12-20 fictional calls with a busy band ('bedient', 'handen in het deeg', 'onder de brug'). The rule for mode 3 is documented in the file header exactly like the Doorlichting wat-als rules: a fixed scripted share of busy-band calls per sector becomes a WhatsApp message; it is a design assumption, not a measurement, and the room says so. Each call lands as a marker: opgenomen (cyan), gemist (dim), via WhatsApp (violet outline). Counters Opgenomen / Gemist / Bericht gekregen / Teruggebeld are aria-hidden during play; one aria-live=polite result sentence is written at the end: 'In deze verzonnen dinsdag bij een {sector} in {district} gingen {n} oproepen verloren toen niemand opnam. In dit scenario kwamen er met een WhatsApp-knop {m} als bericht binnen. Een aanname in een gesimuleerde dag, geen voorspelling over jouw zaak.' No euro amounts, no percentages, no footnotes citing studies. Transforms and SVG attributes only; reduced-motion and lite jump to the end state.
- Databron: Hand-written per-sector scripts in sim-dag-data.js, labelled 'gesimuleerd, fictieve dag' in the subtitle and in the sentence. The visitor's business name is never applied to the day.
- Emotionele taak: Names the fear of the missed 19 u call without threatening; then gives control (flip the mode, watch it change) and relief that the fix is small.
- Conversiestap: 'Zet die knop vandaag op je kassa' deep-links to gereedschap.html#whatsapp?bericht=<urlencoded customer opener per sector, e.g. 'Dag, ik wil graag een afspraak maken. Wanneer past het?'>; the tool labels the field 'Dit is wat de klant naar jou stuurt'. Secondary 'Bespreek dit voor mijn zaak' calls STSBericht.prefill({key:'telefoon'}) with subject 'Antwoord buiten de uren (onderzoek)', the only place an auto-reply text is ever shown.

### Doorlichting: een agent leest de grafieken van een verzonnen zaak en wacht op Snymis (simulatie.html)
- Wat de klant test: How the doorlichting method works on a fictional business of his sector, and what each advice would change in the fictional numbers ('wat als').
- Mechaniek: Existing sim.js engine split into sim.js (timeline, state, UI) + sim-charts.js (four pure renderers). Four fictional businesses (bakker, kapper, garage, new aannemer 'Dakwerken Pauwels, Hoboken'); the chip matching the visitor's sector is preselected; a context line above the stage reads 'Jouw zaak: {zaak}, {gemeente}. Hieronder: de verzonnen {fictieve zaak}. Jouw echte cijfers kennen we niet.' The stage keeps the fictional name. After the three adviezen, sim-whatif.js shows one switch per advice; each has a documented rule in the file header and re-renders the related chart with a second lighter series and a counter 'in deze verzonnen zaak: 23 -> 9 gemiste oproepen per 4 weken', always under the label 'schatting op gesimuleerde data, geen voorspelling voor jouw zaak'. A split-flap phase board (flap.js, driven only by timeline labels) shows VERKENNEN / METEN / LEZEN / ADVIES / WACHT OP SNYMIS. The violet card 'Wacht op goedkeuring van Snymis. Er vertrekt niets automatisch.' closes the run. Pause on hidden/off-screen, reduced-motion skip to end, lite typing speed kept.
- Databron: sim-data.js (three existing fictional businesses) + sim-data-2.js (fourth business + wat-als rules). scripts/qa/sim-check.mjs recomputes every percentage in the findings from the arrays.
- Emotionele taak: Recognition of his trade and control: the simulation literally cannot proceed until a human approves; 'AI' becomes an assistant with a brake.
- Conversiestap: CTA rewritten to what the pipeline really reads: 'Dezelfde blik op wat publiek over {zaak} te vinden is: je Google-profiel en je site op een gsm, als onderdeel van het gratis voorbeeld.' calls STSBericht.prefill({key:'doorlichting', lines:[three advice titles]}). The old 'met jouw echte oproepen' claim disappears.

### Het Google-kaartje om 19 u (gereedschap.html#kaartje-google)
- Wat de klant test: How a customer sees (or does not see) his business on a phone at a given hour, in three states he toggles, using the hours he types himself.
- Mechaniek: Inputs: name + gemeente from sts-zaak, a three-state radio ('Geen profiel' shows a fictional competitor neighbour instead, 'Profiel zonder uren' shows 'Uren onbekend', 'Profiel met juiste uren' shows 'Open · sluit om 18:00'), a range input 12:00-21:00 for the hour, an editable hours table (defaults per sector, Sunday closed). Output: a Google-style local card (SERP styling from tools.css, outlined stars) and one sentence 'Zo ziet een klant {zaak} om {uur} op zijn gsm. Voorbeeldweergave, niet live van Google.' Transform/text updates only.
- Databron: Visitor-typed name, gemeente and hours; sector default hours; no Google request (QA asserts zero external hosts).
- Emotionele taak: Turns the abstract 'vindbaarheid' into the one picture a garage owner understands: being absent at the exact moment a customer looks. Mild fear, immediately fixable.
- Conversiestap: 'Ik wil dat dit juist staat' links to index.html#bericht?onderwerp=kaartje, which composes 'Mijn Google-profiel van {zaak} toont geen of foute uren. Kan dat mee in het gratis voorbeeld?'; secondary link to the Zichtbaarheidscheck.

## Performanceregels

- Three profiles, one table (scene.js header and README): phone (coarse pointer, width < 900) = no Three.js, inline SVG skyline + DOM sign, zero rAF; tablet (coarse, >= 900) = Three.js at DPR 1.0, no bloom, rust 0 frames; desktop = Three.js at DPR min(dpr, 1.5), bloom only when a 60-frame probe averages < 14 ms, rust 0 frames; ?lite, sts-lite, hardwareConcurrency <= 4 and any scene failure = SVG route. Three.js loads only through the inline loader test; no raster poster needs rendering in CI.
- Render-on-demand: states vlucht / settle (600 ms) / rust; rust renders zero frames on every profile (neon flicker only during vlucht/settle); pointer parallax wakes rendering for 400 ms max and only on desktop; QA asserts rAF delta 0 after 3 s idle on desktop and tablet and no loop at all on phone.
- Coverage pause: IntersectionObserver on #scene plus STSScene.cover(fraction) from uithangbord.js; loop stops when > 60 % covered, canvas off-screen or document.hidden.
- The landmark is solid from frame 0 and the city has no progress-driven props; the sign is one 512x128 CanvasTexture on a 12-unit plane updated on input (debounced 120 ms) plus a DOM text element moved only on sts:landed and resize; sector colour is one emissive uniform; raycasting removed (doors are DOM); no per-frame canvas clearing anywhere (cursor trail gone).
- guard() after 150 frames averaging > 28 ms drops composer, DPR, then a second trap swaps to the SVG skyline (sts:scene-degraded); debug exposes frames, longTasks, renderer.info and bloom state, and index.html?debug=1 shows them so the founder can check on the PC that froze.
- Camera flights are 1.2 s GSAP tweens of two Vector3s, interruptible, fixed horizon, never scroll-scrubbed; prefers-reduced-motion turns them into a cut behind a 300 ms fade; no kiosk attract loop.
- CSS: no transition/animation on filter, backdrop-filter, box-shadow, background-position (lint-css + runtime getComputedStyle scan); no backdrop-filter over the canvas at all; no full-page mix-blend-mode; will-change only on transform/opacity during an animation; shadows static.
- Simulations animate transforms, opacity and SVG attributes only; one GSAP timeline each, registered with the room close-hook (pause on leave/hidden, kill on page leave); no setInterval or setTimeout without a clear (wrapped counters in QA); split-flap only on state changes; charts regenerate only on debounced resize.
- Removed GPU work: ASCII readback, comet canvas, liquid feGaussianBlur, animated backdrop-filter tags, .nav blur transition, mesh drift, marquee ticker, Lenis ticker, ScrollTrigger scrub, wire/scan/beacon/belt/trail props, 20 fps ambient rust.
- Budgets measured, then enforced by budget.mjs from budget.json (measurement + 10 %, committed with the commit hash): hard gates are the phone first load without Three (under 450 kB raw in any case), JS per page under 90 kB gzip-equivalent in lite, rAF delta 0 in rust, draw calls at measured + 20 %, zero external requests, zero console errors, zero overflow, all source files under 500 lines (scene files under 300, CSS under 400); the desktop 'with Three' total and SwiftShader long tasks are reported, not gated; the real-GPU Long-Task check (<= 3 per flight) is the founder's ?debug=1 step.
- Rooms on index load their CSS/JS from the page's front matter only; data files (sim-data, sim-dag-data, assistent-dialoog, panden, capabilities, wijken, radar-bericht) are separate from code so content changes never touch the city.
- QA runs 'gl' (headless ANGLE/SwiftShader with --enable-unsafe-swiftshader), 'gl-swift' and 'nogl', on phone 390x844, desktop 1440x900 and tablet 1024x1366, with reduced-motion and ?lite variants in 'gl', against python3 http.server on docs/, with zero console errors and zero requests to any host but localhost as hard gates; the matrix is pruned (gl-swift and nogl only on index, dev/scene, simulatie and one wijk page) and 'npm run qa:quick' exists for iteration; flights under SwiftShader get a 6 s ceiling.

## Criticus (toegepast in deze herziening)

Structurally this is a strong, honest plan: legal pages are kept with only agreed edits, prices are absent, the human-in-the-loop promise stays literal, every simulation keeps a fictional name, existing assets (city, sim engine, preview, tools, contact.js, demos, fonts, config contract) are reused rather than replaced, and most work packages name files and give testable acceptance. It is not yet buildable by headless cloud agents as written. Three things must be fixed before wave 1: the sign (the 'one thing') has no specified size, camera distance or legibility test and will be unreadable at the skyline altitude the plan mandates; WP-03 never says the landmark must be solid from frame 0 and the progress-driven props removed; and the STSBericht.prefill / cross-page conversion contract is created in WP-11 after five parallel packages already depend on it. Add the lint collision on 'jullie', the unmeasurable 'real GPU' Long-Task gate and the raw-byte 1.1 MB budget that fails on day one, and WP-15 cannot go green. On honesty, the body copy is careful but the plan leaks small over-claims at the edges (Kawa proef attribution, sts-world/webshot details, the 'behalve wat je net typte' sentence on card/wijk arrivals, the JSON-LD description nobody owns, preview presets promising agenda integration). On the brief's core complaint, the first screen still reads as a web agency; one extra clause about research, agents and Snymis reading every message would make the first ten seconds tell what STS does without claiming anything untrue.
